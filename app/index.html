App updates

SoundSwap checks for new GitHub releases at startup and every four hours while running. Sound selection works offline.

Download Setup.exe, close SoundSwap, and run the installer. It updates the same installation and preserves your settings, backups and installed game sounds. Updates are not installed automatically.

Version 1.2.0

Original sounds & backups

Apply always starts from the supported original Creative Destruction archive. Unchecked sounds return to their originals.

Your installed file is backed up before SoundSwap changes it for the first time.

Weapon images

Matching cards now use the game images you supplied. Items without a confirmed matching image retain their existing artwork. Right-click a card to choose your own PNG, JPG or WebP image; custom images stay on this PC.

Some items use internal labels until their exact in-game names are confirmed. 92F / P229 share one selection until their independent sound routing is verified.

Included game images

C111 is included here until its sound-bank mapping is confirmed.

About SoundSwap

Apply once, then fully close the app. Sounds stay in the game until you change your selection or restore the original sounds.

Made by sg0hann for switching between BuildTopia and Creative Destruction sounds.

Weapon test badges reflect the results reported in this project. Vehicle and trap additions still need in-game testing. This app does not modify your launcher or anti-cheat.
